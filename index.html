Follow each arrow from the organism being eaten to the organism that eats it. These connected food chains form a food web.

The Science Behind the Magic 🧊

❄️ Abiotic Factors

Freezing temperatures, glaciers, icy fields, crystal mountains, volcanic rocks, geothermal vents, low-to-moderate sunlight, an oxygen-and-nitrogen atmosphere, and warm underground oceans.

🌿 Producers

Aurora Moss, Frost Crystal Algae, and Thermal Bloom make food using sunlight. Some live near geothermal warmth or beneath thin ice.

🐾 Consumers

Primary consumers eat producers; secondary consumers eat other organisms; the Frostfang Wyvern is the top predator.

🍄 Decomposers

Thermal Fungi and Glacier Bacteria recycle nutrients from dead organisms, helping the ecosystem continue.
